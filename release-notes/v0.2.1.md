# The Chat Place 0.2.1

An update to 0.2.0, still a pre-release. When released, installed copies offer this update and keep your sessions and settings.

## Added

- **Agent conversations.** File, New Agent Conversation (Ctrl+Shift+G) puts two chats, Claude and Codex or two of either, in one conversation with you, and passes each one's replies to the other. This is the second phase of chat-to-chat messaging that 0.2.0's Send Reply to Another Chat began. Use your own chats or start new ones, and give each a role, such as builder and reviewer. The conversation is one row in the session list, and its messages list has both agents' messages in order, each naming who said it. You approve each hand-off with Ctrl+Shift+E, or, once you turn it on in Settings, replies go across by themselves up to a turn limit (6 by default). It also stops when an agent says it's done, when the replies start repeating, or when an agent asks you something. Permissions and questions still come to you from each agent, and a message from one agent can never answer or approve anything for the other. (#248) (#347)

## Reporting Issues

Found a problem or have a suggestion? Help, **Report a Bug** fills in a report and shows you exactly what it includes before it goes anywhere: never your session titles, folders or messages.

1. **Open on GitHub** copies the report and opens a new GitHub issue with it filled in. This needs access to the repository.
2. **Copy Report** puts the report on the clipboard, to email to [support@theideaplace.net](mailto:support@theideaplace.net).

## Downloads

TheChatPlace-windows-Setup.exe, TheChatPlace-windows-Portable.zip and TheChatPlace-macos-arm64.dmg, with the update feeds. See the [user guide](https://github.com/TheIdeaPlace/AIChat/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md) to get started, and the [README](https://github.com/TheIdeaPlace/AIChat#readme) for everything The Chat Place does.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac with Apple silicon. For Claude chats: Claude Code, signed in to a Claude subscription. For Codex chats: the Codex app, the ChatGPT app or the Codex command-line tool, signed in to ChatGPT. You need only the one you use.
